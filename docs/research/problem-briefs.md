# Top 3 Problem Briefs

## 1. Competition And Rehearsal Readiness

### Problem Statement

High school show choir students do not have one reliable place to understand what to practice, what changed, what to bring, and what to expect at competitions, so they show up confused, behind, or more stressed than they need to be.

### Who Experiences It

- New students entering competition season
- Returning students balancing a heavy practice schedule
- Any student who misses context shared verbally or informally

### When It Happens

- Before the first competition
- Between rehearsals when changes need to be remembered
- During auditions and seasonal onboarding

### What Goes Wrong

- Students do not know competition flow or norms
- Students are afraid to ask "basic" questions
- Rehearsal changes are easy to forget
- Preparation spills into personal time with no clear center of truth

### Current Workarounds

- Anonymous Reddit posts for basic questions
- Rehearsal videos
- Part tracks and isolated voice recordings
- Long audition handbooks and explainer pages
- Peer reminders

### Why The Workarounds Fail

- They are spread across different channels and files
- They depend on students already knowing where to look
- They do not confirm whether a student is actually "caught up"
- Social friction keeps students from asking when they are lost

## 2. Practice Accountability And Catch-Up

### Problem Statement

Show choir students struggle to keep up outside rehearsal because assignments, part tracks, choreography updates, and corrections are distributed informally, leaving them unsure what they need to review next.

### Who Experiences It

- Students who miss rehearsal
- Students learning fast-changing choreography
- Students who need repetition outside rehearsal to stay performance-ready

### When It Happens

- After each rehearsal
- Before competitions
- During audition prep

### What Goes Wrong

- Students forget fixes
- Students rely on memory and scattered recordings
- The gap between rehearsal time and mastery creates stress

### Current Workarounds

- Part tracks
- Choreography videos
- Watching rehearsal videos repeatedly
- Self-made reminders

### Why The Workarounds Fail

- Materials are distributed as disconnected links and files
- There is no single "do this next" view
- Catch-up depends on personal discipline rather than structured guidance

### Evidence Count

- Reddit: 2 strong/related signals
- Public web: 5 supporting signals
- Indexed X/Facebook: 0 usable pain signals found
- Google Trends: not verified in this environment

### Confidence

Medium-High

## 3. Competition-Day Logistics

### Problem Statement

Show choir students face a dense, all-day competition environment with schedules, finals, solo events, food, parking, and travel details that are difficult for first-timers to understand and easy to miss.

### Who Experiences It

- First-time competitors
- Students traveling to unfamiliar venues
- Students juggling multiple same-day obligations

### When It Happens

- Before and during competition days
- Especially at unfamiliar host venues

### What Goes Wrong

- Students do not know what to expect
- Timing and venue details are easy to miss
- Small misunderstandings become avoidable stress

### Current Workarounds

- Long competition host pages
- Peer explanations
- Group chats

### Why The Workarounds Fail

- They are event-specific and often dense
- They assume students will read or remember everything
- They do not personalize "what matters to me next"

### Evidence Count

- Reddit: 1 direct signal
- Public web: 3 supporting signals
- Indexed X/Facebook: 0 usable pain signals found
- Google Trends: not verified in this environment

### Confidence

Medium

## Recommendation

Problem 1 should be the default build target. The deeper 2025-2026 source pass increased confidence in Problem 1 because recent school and student-publication sources independently show the same pattern: a long season, high workload, and fragmented support materials.

Problem 2 remains a strong backup and could collapse into the same MVP if the product is framed as a student readiness hub rather than a generic practice tracker.
