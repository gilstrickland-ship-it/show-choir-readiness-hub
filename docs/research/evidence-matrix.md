# Evidence Matrix

## Scope

- Window used: March 1, 2024 to March 1, 2026 by default, with a few older supporting posts kept only when they matched recent patterns.
- Geography: U.S.-centric.
- Priority: student-facing problems first.
- Caveat: publicly indexed X/Facebook did not yield usable student pain signals in this pass, and Google Trends was not accessible through this environment.

## Takeaway

The strongest problem is not just "stress." It is a repeated operational pattern:

**students have to piece together what to practice, what changed, and what to expect from scattered materials, informal reminders, and social knowledge.**

That pattern outperforms the alternatives because:

- it appears in both direct student posts and supporting program/director content,
- it is reinforced by multiple 2025 school and student-publication sources,
- it is recurring during auditions, rehearsals, and competition season,
- it already has visible workarounds (`part tracks`, `rehearsal videos`, explainer pages, ad hoc reminders),
- and it is narrow enough for an MVP that can help a student immediately.
